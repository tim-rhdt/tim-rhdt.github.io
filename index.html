About

I’m a doctoral candidate in mathematics at the Technical University of Munich, working at the Chair for Geometry and Visualization. I completed my Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees in mathematics in Munich, with academic visits to NTU Singapore and the Institut Henri Poincaré in Paris. My research interests lie in geometry and mathematical visualization, with a focus on the foundations and implementation of geometric primitive operations in dynamic geometry.

As part of this work, I develop an interactive system for computational and visual exploration of projective geometry, with particular attention to the consistent complex continuation of configurations.

Outside mathematics, you can find me exploring new places with friends and family, in the theater or having a specialty coffee.
