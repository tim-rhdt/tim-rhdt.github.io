Whirling Tiles

The edges of every two-colorable tiling can be oriented consistently. Kites placed along these oriented edges at a fixed but adjustable base angle seem to “whirl” around (parts of) new tiles. Colored like one of the two tile classes, the kites merge with its tiles into a new tiling.

Based on Whirling Tiles by Ulrich Reitebuch, Martin Skrodzki, Henriette Lipschütz and Konrad Polthier (Bridges 2023)
